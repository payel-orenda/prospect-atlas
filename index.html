    Quick guide

    How to use the Prospect Atlas

    StartReading a districtFitFiltersUpdatingMapRegionsHow it's builtWords

    This page shows which California districts to reach out to next, why each one fits, and where it stands with Orenda and Think.

    1. Start at the top of Districts to reach out to. The best fits come first.
    2. Read Why it fits. It's your talking point for the first call.
    3. Click a district to see its details, then set a status and add yourself as owner.

    Reading a district

    Why it fits
    The top reasons this district is a good match, in plain words. The tags underneath are all its signals at a glance, the same ones the Orenda team sees.
    Fit
    Strong, Good, Fair or Low.
    Status
    Where it stands with us, such as Target or Pipeline. Blank means no one is working it yet.
    Owner
    Who is working it.
    Nearest partner or Orenda team
    The closest current Orenda partner or Orenda team member. A nearby partner makes a good reference.

    Current partners aren't in the list. Past partners are, since they're often the easiest to re-engage.

    Fit

    How closely a district matches the districts where Orenda does its best work. Orenda sets the scoring once, so a Strong fit means the same thing to everyone.

    • Strong Reach out first.
    • Good A solid match with a gap or two.
    • Fair Worth a look if you have a reason to call.
    • Low Few signals right now.

    It's a checklist: a district earns points for being on a state support list (25), high need (20), results below what's expected (15), the right size for a state grant (10), being near us (10), an existing relationship (10) and state funding available (10). Click a district to see exactly which points it earned.

    Narrowing the list

    The map always shows the same districts as the list.

    • Search a district or county, either in the box on the map or the one above the list. They're the same search. With 15 or fewer matches, the map zooms in and labels them, and picking a name from the map's list opens that district.
    • Region and Owner narrow to a focus region or to your own districts.
    • Filters are grouped: state support, fit and reach, relationship (pinned by Orenda, past partner, Think Together partner or prospect) and status. Picks in the same group widen the list (DTA or DA); picks in different groups narrow it (DTA and Think Together prospect). The Think Together filters also show their charter schools on the map.
    • Clear all, above the list, resets the search, every filter and card, and the sort.

    Updating a district

    1. Click the district in the list or on the map.
    2. Pick a status: Target (we want to reach them), Pipeline (a conversation is under way) or Not now.
    3. Add yourself as Owner and a short Note.
    4. Click Save. Orenda and Think see it right away.

    For a quick change, use the Status dropdown right in the list. A district that isn't ranked, such as a county office, appears at the bottom when you search for it, marked "Not ranked". Give it a status there to add it to your list.

    To pin several districts at once, tick them in the list (or tick the box in the header to pick every district shown), add a note and click Pin for sales.

    Pinned by Orenda means the Orenda team wants you to see this district, whether it's one to call soon or just an FYI. Their note says why. Click Pinned by Orenda above the list to see only those.

    How this is built

    Data

    • Atlas 2025-26 (ORS): Relative Advantage, the change since 2024-25, SED and EL rates, and enrollment for 883 districts. All public CDE aggregates, with no student records.
    • CDE 2026 LCFF assistance files (Nov 2026): Intensive Intervention status (the DTA list), Differentiated Assistance status, and the full list of 993 districts and county offices.
    • ORS partner list: current and past partners. Use Refresh from ORS to pull the latest. Adelanto is a current partner in the deck but isn't in ORS, so it keeps its deck status with a "Not in ORS" tag.
    • Kept by the team on this page: one record per district (status, note, owner, tags) with a change log, and one settings record (zones, regions, team members, status options, tag meanings).
    • School counts are the ranked schools in Atlas. They can run a little under a district's real count, so confirm the CCEE tier before quoting it.

    Fit score (0–100)

    Fit is a checklist. Each statement that's true for a district adds its points, and Fit is the total. A district's panel lists exactly which ones it earned.

    • On the 2026 state support list (DTA), or an Accountability tag: 25 points. In Differentiated Assistance instead: 15. On the DTA list in 2024 or 2025 instead: 10.
    • High need, 60%+ low-income or 20%+ English learners: 20.
    • Results below what's expected for its students (Relative Advantage −10 or lower): 15.
    • Right size for a state improvement grant, 1–8 schools: 10.
    • Near us, in a focus zone or within the reach radius of a partner or the Orenda team: 10.
    • Relationship, past partner or a relationship tag: 10. A district's status (Target, Pipeline) never changes Fit.
    • Funding available: 5 if eligible for Community Schools (CCSPP) money in 2026–27, and 5 if its 2026–27 Learning Recovery (LREBG) allocation is $125 or more per student. Both amounts show in every district's panel.
    • All-charter district (one of the seven where every school is a charter): −50.

    The points are set once for everyone under Settings → Fit weights.

    Still manual

    These deck factors have no public source. Track them as a status, a tag or a note:

    • Think Together or Think Transcend relationship
    • Funding identified (LCFF S&C, COE dollars, Community Schools Grant, philanthropy)
    • Superintendent champion, or leadership transition
    • Existing partner (Solution Tree, WestEd, Ed Trust West, NCUST, SWIFT)

    Words you'll see

    State support list (DTA)
    Districts the state has picked for its most intensive help. They usually have funding and a deadline to act.
    Differentiated Assistance (DA)
    Districts picked for targeted help with specific groups of students.
    Results below expected
    Students score lower than in districts with similar students.
    Focus zone
    An area where Orenda already works and the Orenda team can easily travel.
    Grant size up to
    The most a district that size can usually get from a state improvement grant: $350K for 1–3 schools, $700K for 4–8, $1.4M for 9–20.
    Heads up
    Something to know before you call, such as a district already working with another provider.
